# US-MACRO-0.3.1 H.15 Source Review

Review date: 2026-10-05<br>
Decision: Candidate semantics and sampled values are equivalent; source amendment is not accepted because the only verified CSV delivery path is scheduled for removal.

## Frozen Slot

The existing `policyRates.realFinancing` slot requires the daily 10-year real Treasury constant-maturity yield, in percent, with a daily observation date. This review does not change its definition, history requirement, transform, weight, threshold, coverage rule, or factor gate.

## Candidate Series

- Publisher: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.15 Selected Interest Rates.
- Native series ID: `H15/H15/RIFLGFCY10_XII_N.B`.
- Exact H.15 description: `Market yield on U.S. Treasury securities at 10-year constant maturity, quoted on investment basis, inflation-indexed`.
- Unit: `Percent:_Per_Year`; multiplier `1`; daily business-day observations.
- History in the reviewed DDP CSV: 6,196 observations, from `2003-01-02` through `2026-10-01`.
- Candidate generated CSV URL on the review date:
  `https://www.federalreserve.gov/datadownload/Output.aspx?rel=H15&series=0b98a66d3ff5e1ea0fbf88adc59b387f&lastobs=&from=2003-01-02&to=2026-10-05&filetype=csv&label=include&layout=seriescolumn`
- The CSV is generated through the H.15 Data Download Program's Build Your Package workflow. The output contains multiple Treasury constant-maturity series; the target column is selected by its exact native identifier and metadata, not by column position alone.

The Federal Reserve publishes H.15 and identifies the source of its inflation-protected Treasury constant-maturity yields as the U.S. Treasury. The H.15 methodology describes TIPS yields adjusted to constant maturities by interpolation from the Treasury TIPS yield curve. Treasury describes its daily real-curve data as par real constant-maturity yields interpolated from its daily par real curve using indicative secondary-market quotations. This is the same economic input required by the frozen slot; `investment basis` in the H.15 description and Treasury's `par real` label were checked against same-date values below.

## Historical Value Checks

The H.15 CSV and the Treasury official daily real-yield XML (`TC_10YEAR`) were fetched independently and compared on identical dates. Every checked value matched exactly at the published 0.01 percentage-point precision.

| Date | Period | H.15 | Treasury `TC_10YEAR` | Match |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2008-10-20 | 2008 | 2.76 | 2.76 | Yes |
| 2020-03-23 | 2020 | -0.04 | -0.04 | Yes |
| 2020-04-01 | 2020 | -0.29 | -0.29 | Yes |
| 2022-06-15 | 2022 | 0.63 | 0.63 | Yes |
| 2022-10-21 | 2022 | 1.69 | 1.69 | Yes |
| 2026-10-01 | Recent | 2.88 | 2.88 | Yes |

This is a semantic/value spot-check, not a claim of point-in-time vintage equivalence. Treasury notes its real-yield curve methodology changed on 2021-12-06 and its TIPS knot-point treatment changed beginning 2008-12-01; those official historical observations were not spliced or adjusted in this review.

## Reuse and Attribution

The Board's website policy says its information is public domain and may be copied and distributed without permission unless otherwise indicated, and requests citation to the Board. H.15 identifies Treasury as the underlying source. A dashboard using an admitted H.15 series should credit both the Board's H.15 release and the U.S. Treasury as the data origin, link to the source, and avoid implying endorsement. The Board's general disclaimer does not warrant accuracy or rights clearance; this source review is not legal advice.

## Delivery Lifecycle Blocker

The exact CSV above is an official DDP `Output.aspx` package generated by Build Your Package (BYP), not a fixed H.15 per-series CSV API. It currently works by requesting the DDP download page and following its generated output link. The official H.15 announcement dated 2026-07-16 says the Board plans to remove BYP the week of 2026-11-09 in preparation for eventual retirement of the DDP. The listed preformatted CSV packages do not include the target inflation-indexed 10-year series; the other official full-release download is a 67.5 MB XML/SDMX ZIP, not the requested durable CSV endpoint.

The direct Treasury daily real-yield XML route and its `TC_10YEAR` field are technically sufficient for a private/research calculation of the frozen real-financing input. That does not resolve the feed-specific public reuse/display basis; public production remains `TREASURY_REUSE_UNRESOLVED`, and no private research route is wired into production.

Therefore, the reviewed endpoint is current and official but not durable for a deployed daily feed. The announcement points BYP users to FRED or the H.15 release XML. Neither is silently adopted here: FRED has separate API-key and user-facing terms requirements, while the release XML needs a separately reviewed, stable per-series extraction path.

## Acceptance

Do not accept a US-MACRO-0.3.1 source amendment yet. Keep `treasury-real-yield` withheld under the existing source-governance state until an official, durable CSV route for the exact series is established. The semantic and historical checks do not justify relaxing any existing eligibility condition, and no Policy/Rates methodology or classifier code is changed by this review.
